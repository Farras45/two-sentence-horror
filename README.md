A two-sentence horror story about a neighbour who tapes a photograph of your front door to your front door every morning — each one taken from a little closer than the last — until the final photograph reveals where it was taken from. A small TypeScript program validates the story (exactly two sentences, at most 400 characters) and writes it to entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
